Hrudhai Lothumalla*, Eshaan Nidee*, Leilani Gilpin — AIEA Lab, UC Santa Cruz
(*equal contribution, co-first authors).

## Idea
Stability selection (Meinshausen–Bühlmann 2010; CPSS, Shah–Samworth 2013) buys
reproducibility and error control with `B = 100–1000` model **refits**. We show
a random forest's **between-tree dispersion** of per-tree attributions is a
strong refit-free proxy for the true cross-resample standard deviation of the
attribution (`r = 0.89` on synthetic ground truth, vs `0.55` for the
infinitesimal jackknife). Combined with **shadow-feature** (knockoff⁺)
calibration, this yields stability-selection-grade FDR control from **one** fit.

## Honest status (findings this session — synthetic ground truth)
- **Dispersion law (SOLID):** between-tree dispersion ≈ cross-resample SD
  (`r=0.89`), beats the infinitesimal jackknife (`r=0.55`). This is the most
  robust contribution and holds regardless of correlation.
- **Single-fit selection @ independence (works):** at `ρ=0`, raw-φ + shadow
  knockoff⁺ gives FDR `0.16 ≤ q=0.2`, power `0.73` vs CPSS `0.66`, at **1 fit
  vs 16** (`14×` less wall-clock). `results/h3_fdr_compute.json`.
- **Correlation breakdown (MAJOR LIMITATION):** with nulls correlated to
  signals, FDR inflates to **≈0.55 for ρ≥0.3** (30 seeds, all sizes) — the
  permutation-shadow null does not preserve correlation structure. The FDR
  claim is credible only in low-correlation designs. `results/grid.json`.
- **Normalization (negative):** selecting by `φ/s` nearly halves power
  (`0.40` vs `0.73`) and lowers reproducibility — dispersion is for annotation,
  not selection.
- **Label-noise robustness:** FDR stays 0; power degrades, collapses by 20%.
- **Scalability:** one fit vs `2B` fits; `10–24×` constant factor.
- **REAL DATA (`results/real.json`):**
  - *Dispersion law validated on real data:* breast_cancer (p=30) `r=0.95`
    (IJ 0.54); Madelon (p=500) `r=0.72` (IJ 0.29) — holds across dimension and
    correlation; beats IJ both times.
  - *Selection under strong real correlation (breast_cancer, mean|corr|=0.40):*
    SF over-selects (FDR 0.78) and **CPSS also fails** (FDR 0.54) — under
    near-duplicate features neither method separates a signal from its copies.
- **E8 — clustering RESTORES FDR control under correlation (`results/group.json`,
  `experiments/group_select.py`):** cluster correlated features, select at the
  group level. Block-correlated synthetic: group FDR stays `0.08–0.16` (≤ q=0.2)
  across ρ=0.5/0.7/0.9 while per-feature FDR climbs to `0.65–0.81`. Real
  breast_cancer: group FDR `0.07` (power 0.90) vs per-feature `0.78`. This turns
  the correlation weakness into a fix (at cluster-level resolution).
